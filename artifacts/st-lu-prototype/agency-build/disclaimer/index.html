Projects depend on your requirements

Timelines, features, costs and outcomes for any project depend on its scope, the choices made, third-party services and the written agreement between you and us. In particular:

  • Descriptions of what a taxi app, dispatch system or white label product can include describe typical options, not a commitment to deliver them.
  • Cost guidance and the scope tools on the website show what influences a budget. They are not quotes or prices.
  • Timelines are agreed for a specific project once its scope is known.
