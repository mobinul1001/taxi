Who we are

This website, TaxiAppDevelopment.org, is operated by Taxi App Development (“we”, “us”). We do not publish a postal address on this website. For privacy questions, Contact us through the contact page.

Information we may collect

We collect only what is needed to run the website and respond to people who contact us. Depending on how you use the site, this may include:

Information you give us

  • Contact and enquiry form details: your name, work email address, company name, type of business, what you want to build, your project stage and the notes you write.
  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address, if you use the form in the page footer. It is sent through Formspree.
  • Anything else you choose to include when you write to us.

Information collected automatically

Web servers and hosting services commonly record technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, pages requested and the time of the request. Our hosting provider, Netlify, may keep such logs. Our analytics tool, Microsoft Clarity, also collects usage information, as described under Analytics and cookies below.

Please do not send sensitive personal information, such as payment card numbers or government identification numbers, through the website forms.

How we use information

We may use the information above to:

  • read and reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible project;
  • prepare scopes, proposals and estimates you ask for;
  • send occasional taxi software news, if you signed up for it;
  • keep the website secure and working, and understand how it is used;
  • meet legal obligations and protect our rights.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis for this use, we rely on the one that fits: steps you ask us to take before a project agreement, our legitimate interests in running and securing the website and replying to enquiries, a legal obligation, or your consent where the law requires it.

Contact and enquiry forms

When you submit the contact form or the quote request, the details you enter are used to respond to you. Fields marked as required are needed for us to reply; the others are optional.

Form submissions are sent to Formspree, a third-party form service, which passes them to us. Formspree handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy, and may keep a copy of submissions in the form’s dashboard, which we use to read and manage enquiries. Access is limited to the people who handle enquiries.

Analytics and cookies

This website uses Microsoft Clarity, an analytics tool, to understand how visitors use the pages, for example which pages are opened, where people click and scroll, and where they leave. Clarity may collect device and browser type, screen size, approximate location (country or region), referring page and interaction data such as clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, and it may create session recordings and heatmaps. It may set cookies or use browser storage to do this. Microsoft processes this data under its own privacy statement (privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

We do not use advertising tools on this website. We do not show a cookie banner. If you do not want analytics, you can block cookies and scripts in your browser settings or with a content blocker.

For details on cookies and similar technologies, see our Cookie Policy.

How we share information

We may share personal information with:

  • service providers that help us run the website and handle enquiries, namely Netlify (website hosting), Formspree (contact and enquiry forms) and Microsoft Clarity (analytics);
  • professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants, where needed;
  • authorities, where the law requires it;
  • a successor business, if our business is sold or reorganised.

Where a provider handles information for us, it should do so only for the purpose we have given it.

Storage and security

We aim to protect personal information with measures that suit its sensitivity, such as serving the website over an encrypted HTTPS connection and limiting access to enquiries to the people who handle them. No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Our service providers may process information in the United States and in other countries where they operate. Where information is transferred across borders, we use the safeguards the law requires.

Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including responding to enquiries, running our business, meeting legal obligations and resolving disputes where applicable. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete information, or to restrict or stop certain uses;
  • object to certain uses, or withdraw consent where we rely on it;
  • ask for your information in a portable format;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, Contact us through the contact page. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it.

Children

This website is written for businesses and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to other websites

The website may link to other sites. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: Contact us through the contact page.
