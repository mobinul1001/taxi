What the booking system does

Taxi booking app features that matter day to day

  • On-demand and scheduled rides

    Book now for a pickup in minutes, or schedule ahead for airport runs, recurring trips and tour pickups. Scheduled jobs are held and assigned closer to the time or by your dispatcher.

  • Fare estimates and pricing rules

    Base fare, distance and time rates, fixed prices for set routes, surcharges and promo codes. Your admin team changes them without a new app release.

  • Driver matching and dispatch

    Nearest available driver, timed offers and manual assignment from the admin panel, with a fallback when a driver does not answer.

  • Payments and receipts

    Card and wallet payments through a gateway you choose, cash where you allow it, and a receipt for every completed trip.

  • Notifications and job links

    Push notifications for passengers and drivers, plus SMS or WhatsApp job links for drivers who work outside the app.

  • Booking history and support

    Every booking, status change and receipt in one searchable record, so your team can answer a customer in seconds.

Behind the Book button

What happens after the passenger taps Book

A booking only works if a driver actually gets it. This is the path every request takes through the system.

  1. Step 1

    Request

    The passenger confirms a booking, now or scheduled.

  2. Step 2

    Match

    Your rules pick the driver: nearest, offered to several, or assigned by a dispatcher.

  3. Step 3

    Offer

    The driver sees pickup, drop-off and fare, and has a short time to accept.

  4. Step 4

    Accept or retry

    If nobody accepts, the request moves to the next driver or to your dispatcher.

  5. Step 5

    Track

    Both sides see live status until drop-off, then payment and rating.
