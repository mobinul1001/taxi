Cost and timeline

What shapes taxi app development cost and timeline

We do not quote from a price list, because two taxi businesses rarely need the same thing. These are the factors that move the scope.

  • Number of apps

    A passenger app, a driver app and an admin dashboard are the usual set. Fewer apps in a first release means a faster launch.

  • Feature scope

    Scheduling, promo codes, ratings and multi-language each add design and engineering work. A clear must-have list keeps the first release focused.

  • Integrations

    Maps, payment gateways, SMS or WhatsApp, and any system you already use are connected during the backend phase.

  • Pricing and dispatch rules

    Metered fares are simpler than zones, fixed routes, surcharges and custom matching logic.

  • Cities and service areas

    One city is the simplest. Several areas with their own pricing and drivers need more structure in the backend.

  • Design depth

    A branded, tested interface takes more design time than a standard layout.

  • Ongoing support

    Store updates, monitoring and new features after launch are planned separately from the build.

Maps, SMS, payment processing and app store fees are charged by their providers and are separate from development.

Development process

How we build a taxi app, step by step

  1. Step 1: Discovery & Strategy

    We learn your market, vehicles, drivers and goals, then agree what the first release must do.

  2. Step 2: UI/UX Design

    Flows and screens for passengers, drivers and admins, reviewed with you before development.

  3. Step 3: App Development

    The passenger and driver apps, built for iOS and Android.

  4. Step 4: Backend & API Integration

    Dispatch, pricing, payments and maps connected to the apps and dashboard.

  5. Step 5: Testing & QA

    Checks across devices, roles and realistic trip scenarios, including failed payments and cancellations.

  6. Step 6: Launch & Support

    Store submission, go-live help and support while your first real trips run.
