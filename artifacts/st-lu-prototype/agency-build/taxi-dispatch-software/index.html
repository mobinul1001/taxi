Dispatch modes

Four ways to assign a taxi job, and when each fits

Taxi dispatch modes compared
ModeHow it worksBest forTrade-off
ManualA dispatcher chooses the driver for each job.Small fleets, special jobs, VIP and phone customers.Full control and judgement, but the dispatcher is the bottleneck.
Nearest available driverThe system offers the job to the closest free driver, who has a short time to accept.On-demand city taxis.Fast response without a person in the loop.
Offer to several driversThe job goes to a group and the first to accept gets it.Busy areas with many drivers.Quick acceptance, with a clear rule for who wins.
Zone queueDrivers wait in a queue for a zone, such as an airport, and jobs go out in order.Airports, stations and ranks.Fair turns and an orderly pickup area.

What you get

The dispatcher console and fleet tools behind it

Dispatcher console

Everything a dispatcher needs on one screen.

  • Live job queue with search and filters
  • Live map of drivers and jobs
  • Assign, reassign and override
  • Create bookings for phone customers
  • Notes and special instructions
  • Alerts for unassigned and late jobs

Fleet management

The records that keep dispatch running.

  • Driver and vehicle records
  • Document expiry alerts
  • Availability and shifts
  • Vehicle types and capacity
  • Driver status: online, busy, offline
  • Trip history per driver and vehicle

Job lifecycle

Every job moves through clear states

Clear states let dispatchers see where each job is, and let the system react when something goes wrong.

  1. New
  2. Assigned
  3. Accepted
  4. On the way
  5. Arrived
  6. In progress
  7. Completed

  • No driver accepts

    The job moves to the next driver or returns to the dispatcher with an alert.

  • Passenger cancels

    The driver is told straight away and the job closes with a reason.

  • Driver drops out

    The dispatcher reassigns the job and the passenger sees the new driver.

Channels into dispatch

Every booking channel lands in one queue

  • Passenger app

    On-demand and scheduled bookings enter the queue automatically.

  • Phone and walk-in

    Dispatchers enter the booking and assign it from the same console.

  • SMS and WhatsApp

    Job links reach drivers who work outside the app.

  • Business accounts

    Company bookings arrive with account details attached.

Control and reports

Measure dispatch so you can improve it

The admin side turns every job into data for managers, so decisions about drivers, zones and rules rest on what happened.

  • Acceptance rate by driver

  • Response and arrival times

  • Cancellations and reasons

  • Driver and vehicle utilisation

  • Jobs by source and zone

  • Document expiry and compliance

Back office

Accounts, invoicing and driver payouts

Dispatch does not end when the job is complete. Operators also need to bill customers and settle with drivers, and the dispatch system is where the data for both already lives.

Customer billing

  • Corporate or account customers with invoiced rides
  • Fare, extras and waiting time recorded against each job
  • Receipts and exportable trip records

Driver settlement

  • Earnings per driver and per period
  • Commission rules for independent drivers and partners
  • Cash and card jobs reconciled separately

Which of these you need depends on how you charge and pay. We confirm it during scoping, and it affects the cost of the platform.
