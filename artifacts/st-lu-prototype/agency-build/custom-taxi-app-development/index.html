Where custom pays off

Situations that call for a custom taxi app

Custom development earns its extra effort where your business differs from the standard taxi playbook.

  1. Fixed pricing for airports and tours

    Zone-to-zone and route-based prices, return trips and waiting time, shown to the customer before they confirm.

  2. Corporate accounts and invoicing

    Company profiles, ride approvals, cost centres and monthly invoicing for business customers.

  3. Dispatcher-first operations

    A console built around how your dispatchers work: manual assignment, outside drivers, job links by SMS or WhatsApp.

  4. Several cities or service areas

    Separate pricing, drivers and rules per area, managed from one admin dashboard.

  5. Special vehicles and passenger needs

    Vehicle classes, child seats, accessibility options and driver qualifications modelled as real rules.

  6. Integration with your existing systems

    Accounting, CRM, telephony or fleet tools connected through their APIs, so your team keeps one source of truth.
