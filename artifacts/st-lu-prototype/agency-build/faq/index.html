Passenger app

What can passengers do in the app?
Typically they set a pickup and destination, see a fare estimate and arrival time, book now or later, follow the driver on a map and pay. The exact list is agreed in scope; the flow is shown on our taxi booking app page.
Do passengers need an account to book?
That is a choice. Accounts give ride history and saved places; a guest option lowers the barrier for one-off bookings. We recommend deciding it with your customers in mind.
Can the passenger app work in more than one language?
Yes, languages and currency can be configured. Each extra language adds translation and testing, which is one of the factors in the cost guide.

Driver app

How do drivers receive and accept jobs?
Drivers go online, receive a request with the pickup and fare, and accept or decline. Depending on your dispatch rules, jobs can also be assigned directly by a dispatcher.
Can drivers upload their documents in the app?
Yes. Driver and vehicle documents can be uploaded for review in the admin dashboard, with expiry reminders so records stay current.
What happens if a driver loses their connection?
Live features need a data connection. How the apps behave when the connection drops, for example how long a job is held, is something we define and test with you.

Admin dashboard and dispatch

What can an admin see and control?
Bookings, drivers, vehicles, fares and reports, with a live map. Which people can see or change what is controlled by roles.
Can I use my own drivers and independent drivers together?
Yes. A dispatcher can assign jobs to in-house drivers or to outside drivers, which suits operators who add partners at busy times. The operator side is covered in taxi dispatch software.
Do I need dispatch software or a booking app?
Many businesses need both: a booking app for customers and dispatch tools for the people running the fleet. They are two sides of one platform, and you can start with one and add the other.

Custom and white label

How do I choose between custom and white label?
Choose white label if your needs are standard and speed matters; choose custom if your pricing, dispatch or integrations set you apart. Both are explained on the white label and custom pages.
Can I start white label and move to custom later?
Often, yes, and many businesses do. The move is easiest when ownership of your data and accounts was agreed at the start, so ask about it before you commit.
Can you build an Uber-like app?
We build ride-hailing platforms with the same kind of marketplace model, under your own brand. See Uber-like app development for what that involves.

Integrations

Which services can the platform connect to?
Common ones are maps and routing, payment gateways and SMS or WhatsApp messaging. Your own systems can be connected where they offer a way to integrate.
Who pays for maps, SMS and payment fees?
Those are charged by their providers based on usage, and are separate from development. Plan for them in your running costs.

Markets and compliance

Can the platform be used in more than one country?
Yes. Languages and currency can be configured, and each extra market adds work such as translation, a local payment provider and testing. That work is one of the factors in the cost guide.
Do you arrange taxi licences or permits for us?
No. Licensing is the operator’s responsibility and depends on where you operate. The platform can store driver and vehicle documents and send expiry reminders, which helps you keep your own records current.
Which payment providers work in my country?
That depends on which providers serve your country and your type of business. We agree the choice with you during the backend phase, and fees are charged by the provider.
Does the platform meet data protection rules such as GDPR?
Which rules apply depends on where your passengers and drivers are. We build with roles and controlled access, and we discuss what your market needs with you. We are not a law firm, so please confirm the legal requirements with your own adviser.
Can one platform cover several cities or areas?
Yes. Pricing, drivers and rules can be set per area and managed from one admin dashboard. The solutions page shows how this fits different markets.

Maintenance and support

What does maintenance cover?
Typically fixes, operating system and app store updates, monitoring and a plan for new features. What is included is agreed in writing, separately from the build.
Can you take over or improve an existing taxi app?
Often. We start with a review of the code and the problems, then recommend fixing, extending or rebuilding parts of it. Our services page lists how we work on each part.

Cost and getting started

Why don't you publish prices?
Cost follows scope, and two taxi businesses rarely need the same thing, so a number without your requirements would mislead you. The cost guide shows what moves it and lets you size your scope.
How can I reduce the cost of my taxi app?
Keep the first release to must-have features, launch in one area, and start from a ready product where your needs are standard.
