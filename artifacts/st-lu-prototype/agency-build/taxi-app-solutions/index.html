Markets

Built for taxi businesses in different markets

The platform is the same idea everywhere. What changes is the setup around it, and the local rules your business has to follow.

  • US

    USA

    Taxi and ride-hailing rules are usually set by the state and the city, and privacy rules can differ by state.

  • UK

    United Kingdom

    Licensing is usually handled by local licensing authorities, and UK data protection rules apply to passenger and driver data.

  • CA

    Canada

    Licensing is usually set by province and city, and privacy rules apply federally and by province. Some regions need French as well as English.

  • AU

    Australia

    Rules are usually set by each state or territory, so a platform for two states may need two sets of settings.

  • EU

    Europe

    Rules differ country by country, EU data protection rules apply to personal data, and several languages and currencies are often needed.

  • CB

    Caribbean

    Rules, currencies and available payment providers can differ from island to island, so these are confirmed market by market.

What we set up for your market

  • Currency and language
  • Payment gateway your country supports
  • SMS and WhatsApp provider
  • Maps and routing coverage
  • Distance in miles or kilometres, date and time formats
  • Driver and vehicle documents and expiry reminders
  • Pricing and areas per city

This is general information, not legal advice. Licensing, tax and data protection rules depend on where you operate. Please confirm them with your own legal adviser. We do not hold or arrange taxi licences for you.
