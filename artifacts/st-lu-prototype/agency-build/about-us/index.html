How we work

Principles we apply to every taxi app project

  1. Scope before code

    We agree what the first release must do, and what can wait, before design and development begin.

  2. Straight answers on cost

    We explain what drives a budget instead of publishing a price list, and give written estimates against a defined scope.

  3. Software you can try

    You see working software in demos and on a staging build, not only slides.

  4. Your brand, your accounts

    We recommend that apps are published under your own developer accounts, and agree ownership in writing.
