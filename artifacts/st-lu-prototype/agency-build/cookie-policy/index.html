What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website can place on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, can also keep information in your browser. They are used to make websites work, remember choices, and understand how a site is used.

How this website uses them

The pages of this website do not set cookies of their own and do not load advertising or social media tracking tools. They do load Microsoft Clarity for analytics, which may set cookies and use your browser’s storage to tell visits apart and to record how pages are used. Microsoft lists the cookies Clarity uses, and how long each lasts, in its Clarity documentation. We do not show a cookie banner.

The St Lu case study (the clickable prototype opened from this website) keeps its demo data and your light or dark choice in your browser’s local or session storage on this website’s domain. It uses no analytics of its own and sends nothing to a server.

Our hosting provider and your browser may still handle technical data, such as server logs, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Types of cookies

Essential cookies

These make a website work, for example by keeping a secure session. This website does not currently need or set them.

Preference cookies

These remember choices such as language. This website does not currently set preference cookies.

Analytics cookies

These count visits and show how a site is used. This website uses Microsoft Clarity for this. It may set cookies such as _clck and _clsk.

Advertising cookies

These are used to show or measure advertising. None are used on this website.

Third-party content

This website does not embed third-party widgets such as maps, video or social media buttons. The Clarity script loads from Microsoft’s servers, and contact forms are sent to Formspree when you submit them. If you follow a link to another website, that site may use cookies under its own policy.

If this changes

If we add or change analytics or other tools that use cookies or similar technologies, we will update this page and say what each is for.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use a content blocker to stop analytics scripts. Doing so may affect how some websites work. Your browser’s help pages explain how.
