The project

From a daily operation to a connected product

  1. The problem

    A St. Lucia taxi and tours operator takes airport transfers, tours and taxi bookings, and needed one place for passengers to book and for dispatchers to run the day.

  2. The solution

    A booking app for passengers, a job app for drivers and a dispatcher panel, designed as one connected product with the operator's own orange brand.

  3. How it was approached

    We scoped the first release around the jobs the business does every day, then designed the passenger, driver and dispatcher screens together so they use the same job states.

What is in the prototype

Three connected parts

  • Passenger app

    Book an airport transfer, tour or taxi, choose a vehicle, pay online and follow the trip.

  • Driver app

    Receive a job, accept it and move it through its stages with one tap each.

  • Dispatcher panel

    Assign in-house and outside drivers, follow jobs on a live map and read the trip timeline.

Features shown

  • Passenger booking with online payment
  • Driver job status updates
  • Dispatcher assigns in-house and outside drivers
  • Live map and trip timeline

How it is built

A front-end prototype built with React and TypeScript. It runs in the browser with sample data and has no live backend or real payments.

Outcome

What this prototype gives the business

A product the owner and drivers can click through before anything is built for real, so the booking flow and dispatch rules can be agreed early and the budget set against a clear scope.
