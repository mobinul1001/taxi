Packages

Choose the package that matches your stage

Prices are in US dollars and indicative. Custom has no listed price: contact us and we quote it after we understand your scope. None of this is a quote or an offer.

  • MVP

    $5,000 to $15,000

    About 3 to 4 months

    One city, one fleet, a first release to test real demand.

    • Passenger app and driver app
    • Core booking, fare estimate and live trip status
    • One payment method and basic notifications
    • Admin dashboard with manual dispatch
    • Staging build, store submission help and handover
  • Most common

    Growth

    $16,000 to $30,000

    About 4 to 6 months

    A working operation that needs automation, more payment options and reporting.

    • Everything in MVP
    • Automatic and zone-based dispatch rules
    • Scheduled rides, promo codes, ratings and driver payouts
    • Several payment methods and richer reports
    • Corporate accounts or a second service area

    • Phased roadmap with a named team

How we quote

From your first message to a written price

  1. Tell us about the business

    City, fleet size, the apps you need and anything you already have.

  2. We agree a first scope

    A short list of must-have features, and a list of what waits for later.

  3. You receive a written quote

    A fixed price for the agreed scope, or a clear estimate with the assumptions behind it.

  4. You decide

    Change the scope, start with a smaller package, or stop here.
