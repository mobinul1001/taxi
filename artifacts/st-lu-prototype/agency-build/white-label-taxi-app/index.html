Before you buy

Six questions to ask any white label taxi app provider

Ask us these, and ask anyone else you compare us with. We agree the answers in writing before work starts.

  1. Who owns what?

    Ask who holds the licence, the source code and your customer and driver data, and get the answer in writing.

  2. Which developer accounts are used?

    Apps should be published under your own Apple and Google accounts, so the listings stay yours.

  3. How do updates reach me?

    Find out how fixes and new versions are delivered and whether they cost extra.

  4. What does support include?

    Ask about response times, who you talk to and what happens during launch week.

  5. What can be changed later?

    Ask what you can customise yourself and what needs the provider's team.

  6. What if I want to leave?

    Ask how you would take your data and your apps with you, and what the exit terms are.
