Feature catalogue

28 features across four parts of the platform

Passenger app

Everything a rider needs to book, pay and travel.

  • Sign-up and profilePhone or email sign-up, saved places and payment methods.
  • Booking flowPickup and drop-off, vehicle choice and a clear fare estimate before booking.
  • Scheduled ridesBook now or for later, including airport pickups at a set time.
  • Live trackingSee the driver arrive on the map and share the trip with someone.
  • PaymentsCard, wallet or cash, with receipts after each trip.
  • Ratings and historyRate the driver, review past trips and rebook a favourite route.
  • Promo codesDiscounts and referral rewards you can switch on or off.
  • NotificationsPush, SMS or WhatsApp updates at each stage of the trip.

Driver app

Tools that keep drivers earning and jobs moving.

  • Driver onboardingProfile, vehicle details and document upload, reviewed by your team.
  • Online and offlineGo online to receive jobs and go offline when finished.
  • Job requestsAccept or decline a job with pickup, drop-off and fare shown.
  • NavigationTurn-by-turn directions to the passenger and the destination.
  • Trip stagesOne tap for on the way, arrived, trip started and completed.
  • EarningsDaily and weekly earnings, trip history and payout status.
  • Driver ratingsPassenger feedback the driver can read and act on.

Admin and dispatch

How your team runs the fleet day to day.

  • Dispatcher consoleA live queue of jobs with search, filters and status.
  • Manual and auto-assignPick a driver yourself or let the nearest free driver be assigned.
  • Live fleet mapEvery driver and active trip on one map.
  • Fares and zonesSet base fares, per-kilometre rates, zones and fixed prices.
  • Driver and vehicle managementApprove drivers, check documents and manage vehicles.
  • ReportsTrips, revenue and driver performance over any period.
  • Customer support toolsLook up a trip, adjust a fare and issue a refund.

Backend

The services working behind every ride.

  • Maps and routingDistance, route and arrival time from a mapping service.
  • Matching and dispatch rulesThe logic that decides which driver gets which job.
  • Payment processingSecure card payments, payouts and receipts through a gateway.
  • MessagingSMS, WhatsApp and push notifications.
  • Roles and permissionsPassengers, drivers, dispatchers and admins see only their own tools.
  • Security and backupsEncrypted connections, access control and regular backups.
